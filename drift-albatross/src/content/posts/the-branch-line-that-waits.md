---
title: The branch line that waits for you
date: 2026-04-22
place: South Bohemia, Czechia
tags: [rail, villages]
summary: A two-carriage train, eleven halts, and a driver who knew which passengers were running late.
---

There is a kind of railway that only survives because nobody has got round to
closing it. A single track leaves the main line, wanders between fishponds and
fields of rape, and stops at halts that are no more than a sign and a bench.

The train is two carriages long. You press a button to open the door and
another to ask it to stop. At the third halt a woman waved from the end of a
lane, still a hundred metres away, and the driver simply waited with the door
open until she climbed in, out of breath, with a basket of eggs.

I got off at a village whose name I had picked from the timetable because it
sounded like a sneeze. It had a church, a pond with ducks, a pub that opened
at four and a bus shelter with a library inside it: a shelf of paperbacks
and a note asking you to bring one back.

The walk back to the next halt took two hours along a dyke between ponds.
Herons stood in the shallows and did not move as I passed. The return train
was the same train, with the same driver, who nodded as if he had expected me.
