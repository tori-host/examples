---
title: Vineyards and a ferry
date: 2026-10-03
place: The Wachau valley, Austria
tags: [rail, rivers, villages]
summary: A local train along terraced vineyards at harvest, and the one crossing that runs on the current alone.
---

The local train along this valley is the slowest way to see it and the only
right one. It runs on the north bank under terraces of vines so steep that
the pickers carry their baskets on their backs, and it stops at every village
that has a church tower, which is every village.

It was harvest week. Tractors with trailers full of grapes queued at the
cooperative, wasps followed them, and the air in the villages smelled of
crushed fruit. Some of the wine cellars hang a bundle of pine twigs over the
door when they are open; I learned that from a sign and then saw them
everywhere.

Halfway along I got off and walked down to a ferry that has no engine. It is
tied to a cable strung across the river, and the ferryman angles the hull so
that the current pushes it over. It is completely silent. A cyclist and I
were the only passengers, and the crossing took four minutes.

On the south bank I walked back through apple orchards to a station on the
other line and watched the north bank go past across the water: the
terraces, the towers, the train I had been on an hour before, small as a toy.
