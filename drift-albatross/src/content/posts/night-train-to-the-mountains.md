---
title: Night train to the mountains
date: 2026-07-08
place: The High Tatras, Slovakia
tags: [rail, mountains]
summary: Six hundred kilometres in a couchette, and a ridge walk that started with the sunrise.
---

The couchette had six bunks and four of us in them. A retired couple from the
coast who had made this journey every summer for thirty years, a young
climber with a rope that took up the whole luggage rack, and me. The couple
had brought a tablecloth.

Night trains are slow on purpose. This one waits for an hour at a junction in
the middle of the night while carriages from somewhere else are coupled on,
and you lie awake listening to the clank and the voices of the shunters and
the long hiss of the brakes.

At half past five the blind was full of mountains. They come up straight out
of the plain, grey and sharp, with snow still in the gullies in July. A
little electric railway runs along their foot, and by seven I was on it, and
by eight I was walking up through dwarf pine towards a lake the colour of
slate.

The ridge path is chained in places, and the wind on top made my eyes water.
I sat by a cairn and watched clouds form below me and drift off over the
valley, and thought about the tablecloth, and how the couple were probably
having breakfast on it by now.
