---
title: Fog on the sandstone
date: 2026-03-14
place: Bohemian Switzerland, Czechia
tags: [walking, rivers]
summary: A morning when the gorge filled with cloud and the path ran out of view three steps ahead.
---

The first train out of Decin was nearly empty: a man with a dog, two students
asleep against the window, and me with a thermos that leaked. By the time we
crossed the river the fog had come up from the water and swallowed the far
bank.

The path climbs out of the village through beech woods and then, without
warning, along the top of the rocks. On a clear day you would see the towers
of sandstone standing in rows like books on a shelf. That morning I saw the
next marker, painted red and white on a pine trunk, and then nothing.

It is a strange way to walk. Every sound arrives from nowhere: a woodpecker
somewhere to the left, water far below, a bell from a village I never saw.
I stopped at a wooden railing at the edge of a drop I could not measure and
ate a roll with cheese while the cloud moved past my face.

Around eleven it lifted all at once. The gorge was there, green and enormous,
and I had been standing above it for an hour. I walked down to the river and
took the ferry back across, the boatman counting coins in a tin box, and the
fog was a thin line high on the rocks, as if it had never been anything else.
