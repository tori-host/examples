---
title: Three borders before lunch
date: 2026-05-30
place: The Zittau Hills, where three countries meet
tags: [walking, borders]
summary: A loop that crosses from one country to another and a third, with no passport and one very good bakery.
---

Where the river makes a lazy bend, three countries share a single point. There
is a stone on each bank and a small monument in the middle, and you can stand
on a footbridge and be, for a second, not quite anywhere.

I started in a town square with a fountain and an argument between pigeons,
walked out past allotments and over a stile, and was in a second country
before I noticed. The signs changed language. The bus stops changed colour.
The bakery did not change at all: it sold the same poppy-seed pastry the
first town had, under a different name and at a different price.

The hills behind are low and round, covered in pines, with odd stacks of rock
at the top that people have given names to: the Owl, the Monk, the Kettle.
From the Kettle you can see all three countries at once, which sounds
impressive until you realise they look exactly the same.

I was back in the first square by one o'clock, three borders and fourteen
kilometres later, with a paper bag of pastry that had crossed them all.
