Our story

Lark Café started as a bread stall on a Saturday market. People kept asking where they could sit down with a loaf still warm, so in the end we found a room for them: a former bookbinder's shop, which explains the shelves. We still bake every morning before we open, and we still close on Mondays, because that is the day the oven rests.
